## Before You Present

Your presentation must cover:

- [ ] The problem and why it matters
- [ ] Your target users
- [ ] The solution you built
- [ ] What you left out of scope and what comes next
- [ ] A demo of the main user flow
- [ ] Your architecture and the reasons behind your key technical decisions
- [ ] At least one trade-off you made
- [ ] One problem you ran into and how you fixed it
- [ ] How you used AI and how you verified its output

Before your session:

- [ ] Rehearse with a timer at least twice
- [ ] Test the demo on the machine you'll present from
- [ ] Prepare a backup recording of the demo
